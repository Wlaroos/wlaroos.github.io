Pixel Buster

Pixel Buster is an arcade rogue-like shoot'em up with a cyberpunk twist. Developed by Kyle Jackson & Mikey Bess.

Current Build

The current build of the game has:

  • 8 playable characters with their own unique starting weapon and ability
  • 20 different weapons, each having unique reloading and shooting animations
  • 3 completed levels with bosses at the end of each level
  • 2 types of shops: one for health and weapons, and the other for stat upgrades

This game was made by me and my friend Kyle Jackson for a few competitions. I am responsible for the majority of the game's content: the entirety of the programming (Unreal Engine Blueprints), and most of the art and animations. Level layouts and the music (by Absolute Valentine) were handled by others.

Programming

Everything in this game was programmed by me from scratch using Unreal Engine's Blueprint system.

The most interesting game mechanics to program were the door and room systems that controlled the flow of the game, the enemies, and the gun/bullet system. The gun system was one of the most complex pieces of code, but once the base was written I could quickly iterate new weapon designs and tweak values while playtesting.

This is the main blueprint for the player's character. Most of this is set up for other functions and to connect gameplay systems to the character.

This is a blueprint for one of the rooms. Collision boxes and variables determine when the character is in the room, which triggers doors and enemy activation. Once enemies are defeated (drops based on luck in enemy blueprints), doors unlock and allow progression.
